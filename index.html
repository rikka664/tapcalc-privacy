2. The core promise: your calculations never leave your phone / 核心承诺：计算不出手机

The App is a calculator. It has no account system, no history cloud, and no backend server. Every number you type and every result you compute is processed in memory on your device and is never stored remotely and never uploaded. The only thing the App persists is a single timestamp recording when you answered the first-launch privacy dialog, kept in the App’s private storage (Android SharedPreferences); uninstalling removes it.

本应用是一款计算器：没有账号体系、没有云端历史、没有后台服务器。 你输入的数字和得到的结果全部在本机内存中完成，不上传、不远程存储。 应用唯一持久保存的内容，是你在首次启动隐私弹窗上做出选择的时间戳， 存放于应用私有存储（Android SharedPreferences），卸载即清除。

3. What the ad SDK collects / 广告 SDK 会收集什么

The App shows advertising provided by Google AdMob (a service of Google LLC): a banner at the bottom of the screen and an occasional full-screen interstitial shown after several completed calculations. To serve and measure ads, Google’s SDK may process the following on your device:

  • Advertising identifier (GAID / AAID) and device model, OS version, screen metrics, language, manufacturer;
  • Network information including IP address, and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • App-open time, session length, ad requests, impressions, clicks and conversions.

The App itself does not collect your contacts, photos, messages, precise GPS location or call log, does not record what you calculate, and does not send any of those to Google or to us.

4. Your choices / 你的选择

  • Consent gate. The Google AdMob SDK is not initialized until you tap “Agree” on the first-launch dialog. If you choose “Decline”, the calculator still works fully and simply shows no ads.
  • Opt out of personalized ads. Reset or disable the advertising identifier in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or use Google’s Ads Settings (myadcenter.google.com).
  • Delete your data. Uninstalling the App removes the single stored timestamp. You can also clear it via Android Settings → Apps → TapCalc → Storage → Clear data.

同意门控：在你于首次启动弹窗点击「Agree」之前，AdMob SDK 不会初始化；选择「Decline」时计算器功能完整， 只是不展示广告。个性化广告退出：可在 Android「设置 → Google → 广告」重置或停用广告标识符。 删除数据：卸载即清除那一个时间戳，或在「设置 → 应用 → TapCalc → 存储 → 清除数据」中清空。
